# Information architecture (plain English)

1. **The answer (top screen)** — One sentence: win mid-size churches first; run a separate named-account plan for large churches. Two big buttons: Mid-size (250–999 weekly attendance) and Large (1,000+). Three-step summary.
2. **Where churches and churchgoers are** — Chart toggling share of churches vs share of churchgoers by size. Ranges and confidence in tooltips.
3. **Mid-size plan** (core focus) — headline facts, then tabs: Win / lose · Who we sell · How we attack · Proof · Open items · Next moves.
4. **Large plan** (Commercial Plus / Enterprise) — same tabs so the two are easy to compare; named accounts, formal reviews, multi-campus, Pushpay and Realm head-on.
5. **What churches value** — chart from 71 coded reviews; giving growth shown at zero.
6. **Competitor explorer** — filter by size; tap a card for their promise, strength, why a church picks us, what not to do.
7. **Open items** — donut by status plus nine plain-English items (R1–R9), each with what clears it and who owns it. Nothing presented as proven.
8. **Review scores** — Clarity 9, Flow 8.5, pass; live mid-size selling on hold.
9. **Ask the plan** — question box (sign-in required; AI off until backend lands).
Always available: top menu, search that jumps to any section. Works on phones; animations respect reduced-motion.
