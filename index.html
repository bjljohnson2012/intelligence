Win mid-size churches first. Run a separate, named-account plan for large churches.

Mid-size churches (weekly attendance 250–999) give the best balance of deal size and time to close, so sales starts there. Large churches (1,000+) hold the biggest share of churchgoers but buy differently — through formal reviews, boards and multi-campus needs — so they get their own plan, not leftovers.
